# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Participation in AsterForge must be respectful, inclusive, and constructive. Examples of welcome
behavior include empathy, useful technical feedback, respect for differing experience and
viewpoints, and accepting responsibility for mistakes. Harassment, threats, discriminatory or
sexualized conduct, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's
private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the private contact channel published
by the repository host. Do not disclose sensitive reports publicly. Maintainers may remove content,
restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary, while handling reports as privately
and fairly as practical. This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project.
